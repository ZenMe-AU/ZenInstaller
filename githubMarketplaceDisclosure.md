# Transparency Disclosure Statement

## Security Measures

ZenInstaller uses GitHub authentication to let users sign in and perform repository and environment setup actions they explicitly request.

Access is scoped to the permissions granted by the user through GitHub. ZenInstaller does not require users to share account passwords directly with the application.

ZenInstaller applies a least-retention approach for sensitive inputs and is designed to avoid server-side persistence of access keys.

## Data Handling

ZenInstaller processes user-provided information only to execute setup and deployment workflow steps selected by the user.

Sensitive credentials, including access keys entered in the app, are collected for in-session use only and are used in ZenInstaller servers for the session but not stored.

User-provided session information is stored only in the user's browser session storage.

ZenInstaller does not sell personal information.

## Data Retention and Transfers

ZenInstaller does not retain access keys on backend systems. Session data retention is limited to the active browser session.

Because ZenInstaller integrates with third-party platforms (for example GitHub, Azure, and AWS) at the user’s direction, data may be processed by those providers under their own policies.

## Compliance and Verification

At this time, ZenInstaller does not claim formal compliance certifications such as SOC 2/3, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or FedRAMP.

If compliance attestations or audit reports become available, they will be published and linked in this disclosure.

## High-Risk Systems (EU AI Act)

ZenInstaller is not declared as a high-risk AI system under the EU AI Act.

ZenInstaller is a deployment/setup workflow tool and does not perform autonomous high-impact decision-making about individuals.
